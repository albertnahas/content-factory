# Content Strategy for Social Media

## Category Psychology

Design content categories around emotional engagement drivers:

| Engagement Driver | Effect | Example Categories |
|-------------------|--------|-------------------|
| **Shock** | Stops scroll, triggers save | Hidden traps, Myth busters |
| **Authority** | Builds trust, triggers follow | Expert tips, Research findings |
| **Aspiration** | Triggers share | Success stories, Premium reveals |
| **Authenticity** | Builds connection | Behind the scenes, Real talk |
| **Utility** | Triggers save/bookmark | How-to, Hacks, Checklists |
| **Curiosity** | Drives watch time | "Did you know", Comparisons |

## Retention-First Design

Every creative decision must prioritize viewer retention:
1. **Hook in 1.5 seconds** — the first frame/sentence determines if they stay
2. **Never front-load the answer** — build curiosity gap throughout
3. **Visual variety every 3-5 seconds** — new shot, angle, or element
4. **Audio engagement** — voice modulation, music drops, SFX at transitions
5. **End with unresolved curiosity** — drives comments and profile visits

## Posting Cadence

| Format | Frequency | Best Time |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| Reels | 4-5/week | 7-9 AM or 6-8 PM local |
| Carousels | 2-3/week | 10 AM - 12 PM |
| Single posts | 1-2/week | Varies |
| Stories | Daily | Throughout day |

## Category Balance

Rotate categories to avoid audience fatigue:
- Never post same category 2 days in a row
- Track category counters per format
- Prioritize "banger" ideas on high-traffic days (Tue-Thu)

## Bookmark Triggers

Content that gets saved (algorithm boost):
- Numbered lists ("5 ways to...")
- Checklists and frameworks
- Comparison tables
- "Save this for later" CTA in caption
